TANK

Guard the golden bird. Stop every enemy tank.

How to Play

  • Keep the golden bird safe. If a shot hits it, the level is lost.
  • Stop every enemy tank to clear the level.
  • Brick breaks. Steel only breaks with a 3 star gun. Water blocks tanks. Trees hide tanks. Ice is slippery.
  • Hit a flashing tank to drop a power-up.
  • Phone: arrow pad on the left to drive, big button on the right to fire. For 2 players, turn the phone sideways.
  • Keyboard: arrows or W A S D to drive, Space or Enter to fire, P to pause. 2 players: W A S D + Space and arrows + Enter.
